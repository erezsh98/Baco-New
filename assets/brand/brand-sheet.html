What changed

The player is now one continuous serve gesture — racket up, ball in play — instead of a static crouch. Letters are geometric and evenly weighted.

Type

Pair the mark with a geometric sans such as Space Grotesk or Outfit for headings, Work Sans for body copy on the site.

Usage

Lime player stays constant; letters flip white on green grounds, court-green on light grounds. Keep clear space equal to the “O” height.
